## Player-Facing 6-Chapter Bridge

## Final 10-15 Minute Scope Lock

Part 44A supersedes the earlier 20-28 minute pacing target with `docs/final-scope-and-pacing-plan.md`. The final player-facing game should be a 10-15 minute birthday gift experience, not a 20-30 minute mini-adventure. This shorter target should come from reducing VN, puzzle, and reveal friction rather than shrinking platformer chapters into tiny routes.

Final target chapter pacing:

| Chapter | Final total target | Platformer target | Pacing intent |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| 1 - The Sealed Envelope | 1.75-2.25m | 75-105s | Complete tutorial chapter with shelf climb/descent and route-awakening ending. |
| 2 - The Hidden Wall | 2.25-2.75m | 105-135s | Tram/stamp/wall chapter with moving tram, vertical wall climb, and slow lift. |
| 3 - The River Witness | 1.75-2.25m | 90-120s | Calm river investigation with bridge up/down movement and witness handoff. |
| 4 - The Archive of Corrections | 2.25-2.75m | 105-135s | Lower archive aisle, file-cabinet climb, upper shelf path, drawer gate/lift, correction descent, and supported silver-key reveal. |
| 5 - The Door of Trust | 2.75-3.25m | 120-150s | Lower courthouse route, Hope/Trust doors, Silver Key ledge, lantern descent, revealed light bridge, wide elevator ascent, and supported blue-ribbon/letter destination. |
| 6 - The Court of the Heart | 2.75-3.25m | 120-150s | True rooftop climb, upper skyline path, roof-gap descent, clue-memory synthesis, forgiving floating ascent, and final-court ceremony, easier than Chapter 5. |

Puzzle and VN target: most puzzles/interactions should resolve in 30-60 seconds. Part 45F-R2 implements Chapter 6 as a fast three-ring final seal moment. Part 44C compresses the active story flow: Chapters 1 and 3 bypass pre-puzzle VN, Chapters 1-5 route puzzle success directly to concise clue reveals, and Chapter 6 routes the final seal directly to FinalVerdictScene.
