# Product Brief

## Premise

Maria and the Case of the Missing Heart is a short browser platformer mystery made as a meaningful birthday gift. Maria, an aplikant adwokacki in Warsaw, follows ten clues through a case that begins like a legal puzzle and gradually reveals itself as a warm, romantic verdict.

## Target Audience

The primary audience is Maria. The secondary audience is the gift giver testing and presenting the game. The experience should feel personal, respectful, clever, playable in a browser, and short enough to finish in one sitting.

## Target Platforms

- Desktop browser.
- Mobile browser.
- Landscape-first mobile layout.
- Static deployment through Vite `dist/`.

## Emotional Thesis

Maria is intelligent, observant, brave, warm, disciplined, funny, and deeply loved. The game should admire her as a whole person.

## Finished-Game Scope

The finished game is planned as a 10-level 2D platformer with short puzzle interludes, total playtime around 15-18 minutes, local progress, final verdict, credits, and mobile-friendly controls.

## Part 1 Scope

Part 1 creates the foundation only: project setup, docs, content data, type definitions, SaveManager skeleton, GameFlow skeleton, tests, and a tiny bootable browser shell.

## Must-Have MVP Features

- Bootable browser app.
- Title screen and opening case file.
- Fixed internal resolution with responsive scaling.
- Data-driven 10-level plan.
- Typed puzzle plan.
- Save and flow skeletons with unit tests.
- Clear Codex workflow for future parts.

## Must-Not-Do List

- Do not build the full game in Part 1.
- Do not implement all 10 levels yet.
- Do not add final art, music, photos, or private memories yet.
- Do not create a complicated engine.
- Do not add unrelated mechanics or external assets.
